# Employee Handbook
**BlueMoon Fashion**

## 1. Welcome to BlueMoon!
Welcome to BlueMoon Fashion. We are committed to maintaining a transparent company culture and delivering the best performance in the industry.

## 2. IT & Security
All employees must adhere to our security policies. Passwords should be changed regularly and must not be shared. 
For onboarding issues, please contact the IT team.

## 3. Remote Work Policy
Remote work is allowed up to 3 days a week. Please ensure your VPN is connected at all times when accessing internal portals.
